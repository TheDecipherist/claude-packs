# Type, space and colour: a few strong choices, measured

Numbers from taste-skill's study of 247 gallery sites, unless marked.

<constraints>
Always do:
- Type: two or three sizes do the work, five is the ceiling. One family is normal, two the limit. Body text 16-20px, line height 1.45-1.6, 45-75 characters per line. A real scale: a headline-to-body ratio around 4.3 on the studied sites.
- Space: one grid and one pair of margins, equal on both sides. The gap between sections is clearly larger than any gap inside one, and spacing has rhythm: tight inside a group, loose between groups.
- Headings: one `h1`, no skipped levels, more space above a heading than below it.
- Colour: one ground, one ink, one accent, the accent on the action and perhaps one other thing. Running text in the full ink. Separate surfaces by a small step in tone.
- Align left by default; centre at most one moment.
- Align everything horizontally and vertically. What looks grouped is a group: the same size, the same spacing, shared edges. Repeated things are one size.
- Name colours as tokens by role: ground, surface, ink, muted, accent, accent ink. A raw hex in a component is a second palette starting.
- Headlines: size contrast is the lever, not weight. Half of the studied heroes are regular weight.
- Stay above the floors: text at 12px or more, contrast 4.5:1 for body and 3:1 for large text (WCAG).

Never do:
- Justify narrow columns, or set long passages in capitals.
- Put grey text on a coloured background: use a darker shade of that colour.
</constraints>
