# Instances of one thing sit in a selector grid, told apart by header or cell

Two filters are identical twins; a colour that tells them apart breaks the moment the colour is changed or not seen.

<constraints>
Always do:
- Show instances of one thing (LFO 1-4, ENV 1-4) in a selector grid with activity arcs.
- Tell instances apart by their header text or their grid cell.
- Carry the tab conventions of `panel-tabs` into the grid: one row, the active cell in orange.

Never do:
- Tell two instances apart by colour.
</constraints>
