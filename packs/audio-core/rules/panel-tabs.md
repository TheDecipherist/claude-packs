# Tabs sit in one row under the panel's title, the active one in orange

Tabs in one place, marked one way, are found without looking.

<constraints>
Always do:
- Put tabs in one row under a panel's title, the active one in orange.
- Keep one tab per page, named for what the page holds.

Never do:
- Put tabs in a second row, or mark the active tab by anything but its colour and shape.
</constraints>
