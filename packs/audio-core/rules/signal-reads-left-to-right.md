# On an original, a panel's layout follows the signal

A reproduction keeps the machine's own positions (`reproduction-or-original`); our own designs read in one direction.

<constraints>
Always do:
- Lay the signal out left to right, the master output rightmost with its meter.
- Put the display top-centre, and modulation in its own panel.

Never do:
- Move a reproduction's sections to fit this.
</constraints>
