# A collapse never hides a route

<constraints>
Always do:
- Ask what else was reachable only through the half that folds.
- Keep every control a pack contributes reachable.
- Read a control's real rectangle after layout.
- Make every state escapable by the control that entered it: a fold opened by a button closes by that button.

Never do:
- Trust the flags: a headline control once came out zero pixels tall, with every flag correct.
</constraints>
