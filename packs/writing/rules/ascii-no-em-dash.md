# ASCII only, and never an em dash

The cleanest discriminator there is: em dashes are in 0-0.5% of human posts and 97% of LLM-flagged ones.

<constraints>
Always do:
- Use a comma, a parenthetical or a period instead. Parens are the natural aside (half of real posts have one) but don't prove humanity; slop overuses them too.
- Keep to ASCII: straight quotes, three dots, a plain hyphen.

Never do:
- Write an em dash, or " -- " in its place.
</constraints>
