# Write from your own corner, end on the ask, hedge like a person

Measured, as defaults.

<constraints>
Always do:
- Write in first person from your own situation (about 90% of human posts), with oddly specific numbers ("200k users", "$900", "30/100"). Humans run 2.8x more first person than second; slop is near 1:1 and lectures "you". Round numbers read fake; specific ones read lived.
- End on the question or a bare thanks. Comments just stop, often without terminal punctuation.
- Keep self-anchored doubt where it is real: "not sure if this is a good idea tho", "afaik". A quarter to a third of real posts do it, and slop hedges LESS (it performs confidence).

Never do:
- Write a closing thought or a summary close: measured at about 0%.
- Hedge symmetrically ("it depends on your use case"), or both-sides a claim Tim holds.
</constraints>
