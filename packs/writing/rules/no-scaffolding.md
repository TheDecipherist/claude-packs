# Scaffolding is the slop shape; a real list is short and ragged

Bullets do not kill posts (human posts with bullets slightly outscore those without, 4 vs 3); scaffolding does. Bold is in 5% of human writing vs about 30% of slop, headers in under 2%.

<constraints>
Always do:
- Put receipts as specific numbers woven into prose, or one short ragged list: uneven item lengths, sometimes two items. When winners use bullets it is ONE short list of one-liners (top 20 text posts: 4 of 20 in r/ClaudeAI and r/ClaudeCode, 8 of 20 in r/mongodb, 6 of 20 in r/webdev and r/node; headers 2-4 of 20; median 140-240 words).
- Write the rest as prose in short paragraphs. A 90-word bullet is a paragraph with a dash in front; make it prose.
- On a manual page, put no heading directly under the title; the first heading comes where the subject changes.

Never do:
- Bold-header and numbered-bold skeletons, outside articles whose own guidelines require structure.
</constraints>
