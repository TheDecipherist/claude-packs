# No cargo cult: these rules are about cadence, not a word list

<constraints>
Never do:
- Ban common English words ("that", "just", "actually"): the bans break normal English.
- Swap one canned phrase list for another; replacements become tells too.
- Add bullets, an FAQ or headers to "sound organized". Real lists are fine and ragged.
- Turn every hedge into a confident claim. Verify, then state it flat: manufacturing confident wrong statements is worse than sounding like AI.
- Force casualness. Measured slop is already chattier and more contraction-heavy than real posts; piling on "super", "totally" and exclamation points is its own tell. Humans mix registers.
</constraints>
