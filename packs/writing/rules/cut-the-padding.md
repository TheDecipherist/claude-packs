# Cut about half: say the thing, one concrete consequence, stop

AI-ness is not in individual words; it is in cadence: padding, symmetry, hedging-by-committee, over-explaining. Fix rhythm and structure, not vocabulary. When a real sample of the target register exists, match it.

<constraints>
Always do:
- Cut about half of a normal AI draft. Say the thing, name one concrete consequence, stop.
- Write one vivid concrete line, not three explanatory sentences.
- Use the verb: "make a determination" is determine, "provide an explanation" is explain.
- Calibrate (measured): the median real comment is under 50 words, the median real post 100-125, LLM-flagged posts 230. A reply of more than a few tight sentences is probably too long. For a post the sub's norm wins: a 300+ word value post is fine and scores well. Cut padding, not substance.

Never do:
- Open with a wind-up or "great question", or restate the question.
</constraints>
