# Decide whether to reply at all

The author does NOT reply just to be right. A reply must make a difference.

<constraints>
Always do:
- Wrong + few upvotes: ignore. Going nowhere.
- Wrong + highly upvoted + clear why: reply. The reply is for the silent many who upvoted the misinformation, not for the author.
- Right but adds nothing, or low visibility: skip.
- Merely smug, no spreading misinformation: skip; replying would be ego.
- When surfacing comments for the author, filter OUT "technically wrong but changes nothing".
- Whenever we do reply: tear down AND redirect. Give the correct approach.

Never do:
- Leave someone with just "no".
</constraints>
