# A feature gets the control travel its measured audibility earns

Travel spent on a law no player can hear leaves less for the steps that matter.

<constraints>
Always do:
- Before a feature takes travel on a control, measure what each step removes and from which level.
- Place the feature where a player hears the change.
- Drop a measured law no player can hear.

Never do:
- Give a feature travel because it was measured, when the measurement shows it inaudible.
</constraints>
