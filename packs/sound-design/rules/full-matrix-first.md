# The engine carries the machine's whole modulation matrix the day its offsets land

A matrix of N destinations by M sources folded into one depth cannot be unfolded later without rewriting every patch that used it.

<constraints>
Always do:
- Give every destination and source pair the machine has its own depth in the engine. The panel may expose part of the matrix.
- Ask, for every modulation source, whether it runs per voice or per bank, and write the answer where the source is defined.

Never do:
- Collapse N destinations by M sources into one depth.
</constraints>
