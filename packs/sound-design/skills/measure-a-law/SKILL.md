# Measure a law (one job: a fit you can defend)

<constraints>
Always do:
1. Use a sine at the frequency you read. A filter slope read on a saw was off by 2.3 dB per octave.
2. Write a filter's law in the units its procedure uses.
3. Fix it with two anchors.
4. Keep fitted constants scripted, tagged, and rerunnable.
5. Write disproofs down.

Never do:
- Fit a single point.
</constraints>
