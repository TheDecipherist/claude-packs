# Read the bank for a missing term (one job: find a missing term by where the whole bank settles)

A term that is zero at rest passes every resting check and still shapes every program that sets it. Render the whole bank and the outliers show where it hides.

<constraints>
Always do:
1. Render every factory program, and tabulate each computed value's settled figure: the share of programs under a floor, the share on a clamp, and the median.
2. Flag programs that are implausibly dark or bright, or bunched on a limit.
3. When the resting check is clean, count how many programs set the byte: a term that is zero at rest hides there.
4. Name the suspect term, add it, render the bank again and show the outliers move.

Never do:
- Judge a bank from the INIT or from a few programs picked by ear.
- Add a term to fix an outlier without the count of programs that set it.
</constraints>
