# Read a patch book (one job: turn a book of drawn knobs into values)

A printed book is a corpus. Its knobs are pictures, so the values come from the pointer angles, and the match to decoded bytes is a correlation to be stated with its margin.

<constraints>
Always do:
1. Find each drawn knob and switch with a ring template, and read its pointer angle. Keep the template per knob style.
2. Rank-correlate each drawn knob against every decoded channel and assign them one to one, stating the margin to the runner-up. Count the facsimile as a second source only for its margin on programs not used to choose the mapping (`two-sources-before-naming`).
3. Count a control only over the programs that route it: an unused knob is drawn at zero.
4. Take program numbers and names from the unit's own manual, and check the page count against the decoded bank.

Never do:
- Read a knob drawn at zero as a decoded zero.
- Take a rank tie as an assignment.
</constraints>
