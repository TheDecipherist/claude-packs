# Leave the record (one job: the next reader can check every claim)

<constraints>
Always do:
1. The research record, with withdrawn findings in place.
2. CSV maps of every parameter, with provenance.
3. The laws reference: each law, its source, and where the code is.
4. The debt ledger of `[EAR]` values, with leads.
5. Tests, including a byte-exact round trip of every patch.
6. The non-negotiables list.
7. State which tags cannot exist here, and which check is lost with no corpus.

Never do:
- Leave a verdict with no number behind it.
</constraints>
