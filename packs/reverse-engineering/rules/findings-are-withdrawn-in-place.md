# A finding is withdrawn in place, with the same evidence a claim needs

<constraints>
Always do:
- Mark a withdrawn finding where it stands, with what overturned it.
- Hold a withdrawal to the bar of a claim: run the mapping over the whole table, and every source again.
- Treat a flat distribution as a sign the decode is wrong.
- Follow a withdrawal to everything built on it: code that still includes the table, and other repos' copies of the finding.

Never do:
- Delete a wrong finding: the next reader will make the same mistake.
- Withdraw on two rows.
</constraints>
