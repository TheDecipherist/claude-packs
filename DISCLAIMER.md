## Reverse engineering is for learning and interoperability

Several packs, and the work order templates, teach reverse engineering: mapping firmware, reading circuits, comparing a rebuild with the machine it rebuilds. Use them to learn how a thing works and to make your own work interoperate with it.

- The laws on reverse engineering, and on what may be done with what it finds, differ between countries and between licences, end-user agreements and terms of use. Check what applies to you before you start, and again before you publish anything you made from it.
- Facts about how something works are not the same as its code. The kit's own laws say: take facts, cite them, prove them, and never copy another project's code. Before you use any code you have read, in firmware, a decompiler's output or another project, check that you are allowed to.
- A rebuild of a product is your own work only when its code is your own. Do not ship another maker's firmware, assets or code inside it unless their licence allows it.
