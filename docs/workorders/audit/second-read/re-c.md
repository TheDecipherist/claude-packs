## 4. Top five

1. **#2, direction and kind of every stored value**: three machines in this slice were bitten (DX7 velocity, Matrix-1000 rate tables, JX-8P ladder), and an inverted law passes range checks and the corpus.
2. **#4, identify the checksum and bit order from the carrying part**: without it a disk or container stays "noise" for as long as no one guesses the polynomial.
3. **#5 and #6, descramble before diff, and a part can hold two things**: both made a wrong "real revisions" and "service ROM" call until measured, and the kit scores neither.
4. **#13, name the mechanism, then write its consequences for the engine**: shared bank modulation, divisor quantisation, saturated feedback and hold plus tracking filter each changed how the engine is built, not a number in it.
5. **#7, release notes against the diff**: it turns a version diff into named words and flags hunks nobody explained.
