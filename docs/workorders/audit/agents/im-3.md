## New skills (scenarios)

- read-the-bank-for-a-missing-term. Fire: a rebuilt bank passes every resting probe yet 40 programs sound dull; render all, tabulate share under floor and on clamp, flag outliers, count programs setting the suspect byte. Near miss: finding what an unknown byte does (identify-a-parameter). A followed run renders the bank, tabulates, flags, counts setters, adds the term and re-renders.
- derive-preset-values. Fire: a reverb bank decodes with 0 percent wet in every program and the manual lists names and keywords. Near miss: choosing a recipe from statistics (generate-a-recipe). A run drops the decode per parameter, takes range from keywords, name-picked point tagged [EAR], writes laws.md.
- measure-unison-detune. Fire: a super saw's detune fitted from a spectrum peak comes out 0. Near miss: a filter corner (measure-a-filter). A run takes f0 by autocorrelation, reads low harmonics, gives each partial its own peak, normalises to the centre partial, plants a known stack.
- measure-a-modulation-effect. Fire: a chorus's delay and width are to be compared with a recording. Near miss: reverb decay (measure-reverb). A run reads the autocorrelation of the power spectrum, watches the peak move, compares channels, proves on a planted delay.
