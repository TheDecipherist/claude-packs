## 3. Verdicts

- **firmware-recon: PARTLY IN THE KIT.** Names, wrappings, interleave, house-tool placement, two-sources, provenance and render-not-OCR are in. MISSING: dump triage (F1-F5), CPU identification and the vendor-name trap (F10, F11), Cortex-M and C2000 notes (F13, F14), proven-boundary and encoding-counting (F15-F16), timer discovery (F19), DSP carve (F22), analysis order (F24). Not safe to disable until those land.
- **patch-decode: PARTLY IN THE KIT (thin).** Names-first covers stride, count and range-over-printed; nearly all of the decode method is absent: id-as-label-index, signatures and switch test, per-parameter scaling, flag bits, ratio proof, bank-wide attribution, byte-sum "done" (P1, P3, P6-P8, P10-P19). Already `disable-model-invocation: true`, but the content is not elsewhere; not safe to retire.
- **tempo-sync: PARTLY IN THE KIT.** The clock law (midi-law sections 1-9a) is mostly in `clock-law.md` and the midi rules. MISSING: SYNC-INT-default law and its probe (T1, T2, with a conflict against the kit's off-by-default rule), two clocks, hardware-vs-ours, fourth state, tick table, row-by-row list, stopped host, one switch, and the whole firmware-time section (timer, note twin, flag, ratio, scaling). Not safe to disable.
