Context finding: the kit draws the panel IN CODE from a control table and forbids sprites, images and an HTML prototype (`ui-drawn-in-code`, `no-html-prototype`, rebuild.md W13). The synthpanel-creator methods (native export, sprite cache, themes JSON, `panel:native`, bindings.json `kind`) therefore do not carry over as such; rows marked **N/A (design)** are that. Only the lessons under them are counted.

Rows 21 gives a source-ranking conflict between rebuild W2.4 and `analyse-reference-look`.

**Safe to disable once the kit holds rows 1 to 4, 6 to 9, 11 and 12 and the row 21 conflict is settled.** Not safe now: it is the only place the photo-quality test, the aspect table and the mode-key checklist live.
